# Brickfront development order

1. Combat clarity now: held weapon models, shared muzzle positions, muzzle flashes, short tracers, red hit direction and replay parity.
2. Gameplay together: visual-only AI perception and reaction delays, longer survival against automatic fire, reliable rocket direct hits and area damage.
3. Halo-inspired custom matches: primary/pistol/launcher inventory, weapon pads and replacement, host-owned weapon presets synchronized to all players.
4. Verify the actual game and publish the working update.
5. Later art pass: select one small Baghdad section and bring brick proportions, bevels, plastic materials, lighting, destruction, animation and audio to the intended quality before spreading that treatment across both maps. Keep collision and match logic separate from render assets.

Avoid baking expensive final assets into mechanics that are still changing. Graphics that explain combat are required now. Richer environments follow a proven match loop. Breath sounds remain deferred by request.

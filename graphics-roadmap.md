# Brickfront graphics roadmap

Keep the current game as the graybox. Build combat clarity and enjoyable matches together, then invest in the full art pass.

1. Combat clarity, implemented in this update: visible held weapons, barrel-origin shots and muzzle flashes, short tracers, red directional hit feedback, and weapon information in the HUD.
2. Playtest the match loop: fair visual AI, movement, time to kill, pickups, sniper restrictions, and actual private-room matches. Tune these before making every corner expensive to change.
3. Polish one Baghdad street as the visual benchmark: detailed studded brick models, stronger silhouettes, soldier grips and animations, richer plastic materials, lighting, damaged vehicles, and debris. Test it with eight combatants and split-screen.
4. Apply the proven style to both maps. Add the audio pass, including breathing feedback, after the damage states feel right.

Textures alone will not produce the intended visual jump. Model shapes, animation, materials, lighting, effects, and performance all matter.

## Suggested next playtest

- Watch an enemy fire, then inspect the killer's weapon in the replay.
- Break line of sight at a corner and relocate. A bot should investigate its last sighting rather than track your hidden movement.
- Carry a primary, a pistol, and a launcher. Use 1–3 to switch and X to replace a slot at a pickup.
- Host Snipers Only, then have a friend join and confirm the same restrictions.
- Hit two close enemies with one rocket. Compare the result with regular rifle body hits.
- Check the red damage vignette and its direction marker.
